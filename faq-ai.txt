# Frequently Asked Questions - 816800.xyz

Lang: en
Website: [https://816800.xyz/](https://816800.xyz/)
Last Updated: 2026-09-15

[About the Site]

Q: What is 816800.xyz?
A: A free, static toolkit of browser-based tools for developers and creators.
   Five tools are live: an M3U8/HLS and DASH stream tester, a URL shortener, a
   case converter, a word counter and a Unicode text styler. There is no
   account system, no upload step and no tracking.
URL: [About 816800.xyz](https://816800.xyz/about/)

Q: Is 816800.xyz free?
A: Yes. There are no usage caps, no watermarks, no trial period and no paid
   tier. The site may display advertising in future to cover hosting costs, but
   no feature is behind a payment or a login.

Q: Who runs 816800.xyz?
A: A small independent project. The content carries no named author, and the
   site does not publish a company identity or a team page. Enquiries go
   through the contact page.
URL: [Contact](https://816800.xyz/contact/)

[Tools]

Q: Which tools are available?
A: Five: the M3U8/HLS player, the short link generator, the case converter, the
   word counter and the fancy text generator. Cards marked "coming soon" on the
   homepage describe tools that are not built yet.
URL: [All tools](https://816800.xyz/tools/)

Q: Does anything I type leave my device?
A: No. Text conversion, counting and font mapping run in the browser tab. The
   only network traffic is the stream segments you asked the player to fetch,
   which come directly from their own server rather than through this site.

Q: Do I need to install anything or sign up?
A: No. No installation, no account, no email address. If the browser can open
   the page it can run every tool on it.

[Streaming]

Q: What can the M3U8 player do?
A: It plays a .m3u8 or .mpd URL in the browser using hls.js or dash.js, and it
   diagnoses failures by separating "URL unreachable" from "blocked by CORS" —
   two errors that look identical on screen but need opposite fixes. It also
   keeps a local history of the last few URLs tested.
URL: [M3U8 / HLS player](https://816800.xyz/m3u8-player/)

Q: Which streaming formats does it support?
A: HLS via .m3u8 playlists and MPEG-DASH via .mpd manifests. Codec support
   follows the browser: H.264 with AAC universally, HEVC reliably in Safari,
   AV1 in recent Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

Q: Can it bypass DRM or regional restrictions?
A: No. It cannot play DRM-protected streams, and it cannot bypass
   authentication, geographic restrictions or paywalls. If a stream returns 403
   it needs a token, a Referer or a signed parameter supplied by its owner.

[Short Links]

Q: How does the short link tool work?
A: It encodes the destination URL into the link itself and serves a static
   redirect page. No server-side database stores the mapping, so there is
   nothing to leak and no link to expire server-side.
URL: [Short link generator](https://816800.xyz/short-link/)

[Limits and Support]

Q: Is there an API?
A: No. There is no public API, and no downloadable application. Every tool is
   meant to be used through the page.

Q: Something is broken. Who fixes it?
A: Send the tool name, the input you used and your browser through the contact
   page. A reproducible report is the fastest route to a fix.
URL: [Report a problem](https://816800.xyz/contact/)
