什么是 HLS？为什么浏览器不能直接播 m3u8 What Is HLS? Why Browsers Can't Play M3U8 Directly

Last updated: September 19, 2026

2026-08-19 · 5 min read

一、HLS 是什么

HLS（HTTP Live Streaming）是 Apple 在 2009 年推出的自适应流媒体协议。它的核心思想是用普通的 HTTP 把视频切成很多小片段来传输，从而天然享受 CDN、缓存和防火墙的友好性。今天 iPhone、Mac 上的视频几乎都走 HLS。

二、m3u8 与 .ts 的关系

  • .m3u8：播放清单（Playlist），纯文本，列出各档位（清晰度）的子清单或分片地址。
  • .ts（Transport Stream）：实际音视频分片，通常 2~10 秒一段。

播放器先下载 .m3u8，解析出分片列表，再按顺序拉取 .ts 拼成连续视频。

三、为什么浏览器不能直接播 m3u8

浏览器内核只认 MP4 / WebM 这类「封装好的媒体文件」，并不理解 HLS 的清单+分片模型。直接把 .m3u8 丢给 <video> 标签，浏览器会一脸茫然。必须由播放器在中间做「翻译」：用 MSE（Media Source Extensions）把分片喂给视频元素。

四、hls.js 做了什么

本站播放器内置的 hls.js 就是干这个的：它下载并解析 m3u8，按网络状况选档位，把 .ts 转封装后通过 MSE 推给 <video>。所以你粘贴地址就能播，全程在浏览器端完成，不上传任何数据。

五、HLS 与 DASH

两者理念一致（清单 + 分片 + 自适应），阵营不同：HLS 由 Apple 主推，清单用 .m3u8 / 分片用 .ts；DASH（MPEG-DASH）是国际标准，清单用 .mpd、分片多用 .m4s。本站同时内置 dash.js，粘贴 .mpd 也能直接播。

1. What HLS is

HLS (HTTP Live Streaming) is Apple's adaptive streaming protocol, introduced in 2009. Its core idea is to ship video over plain HTTP by cutting it into many small segments, which naturally plays well with CDNs, caching and firewalls. Almost all video on iPhone and Mac uses HLS today.

Apple publishes the authoring side as the HTTP Live Streaming documentation; the wire format itself was later formalised as RFC 8216. Those two documents are the source of every statement in this guide.

2. The relationship between m3u8 and .ts

  • .m3u8: the playlist — plain text listing the sub-playlists (bitrates) or segment URLs.
  • .ts (Transport Stream): the actual audio/video segments, usually 2–10 seconds each.

The player downloads the .m3u8, parses the segment list, then fetches the .ts files in order to assemble continuous video.

3. Why browsers can't play m3u8 directly

Browser engines only understand "packaged" media like MP4 / WebM; they don't understand the HLS playlist-plus-segments model. Hand a .m3u8 straight to a <video> tag and the browser is lost. A player must sit in the middle as a "translator", feeding segments to the video element via MSE (Media Source Extensions).

4. What hls.js does

The player on this site bundles hls.js for exactly this: it downloads and parses the m3u8, picks a bitrate by network condition, transmuxes the .ts and pushes it to <video> via MSE. So you paste a URL and it plays — entirely in the browser, uploading nothing.

5. HLS vs DASH

They share the idea (playlist + segments + adaptation) but differ in camp: HLS is pushed by Apple (.m3u8 / .ts), while DASH (MPEG-DASH) is the open standard (.mpd / .m4s). This site also bundles dash.js, so pasting a .mpd plays directly too.

6. What a real m3u8 file looks like

The fastest way to understand HLS is to read one. A media playlist — the one that actually lists segments — looks like this:

#EXTM3U
#EXT-X-VERSION:3
#EXT-X-TARGETDURATION:10
#EXT-X-MEDIA-SEQUENCE:0
#EXTINF:10.000,
segment0.ts
#EXTINF:10.000,
segment1.ts
#EXTINF:7.500,
segment2.ts
#EXT-X-ENDLIST

Reading it top to bottom: the file declares itself HLS, targets roughly 10-second segments, starts at sequence number 0, lists three segments with their exact durations, and closes with #EXT-X-ENDLIST — which means the list is final and the content is on demand.

A master playlist is one level up. It contains no media itself, only pointers to variants at different bitrates:

#EXTM3U
#EXT-X-STREAM-INF:BANDWIDTH=800000,RESOLUTION=640x360
360p/index.m3u8
#EXT-X-STREAM-INF:BANDWIDTH=2400000,RESOLUTION=1280x720
720p/index.m3u8
#EXT-X-STREAM-INF:BANDWIDTH=5400000,RESOLUTION=1920x1080
1080p/index.m3u8

The player starts on a middle rung, measures how fast segments arrive, and climbs or drops the ladder as conditions change. That switching is the whole point of adaptive streaming.

7. HLS tag cheat sheet

The tags you will actually meet when debugging a playlist
TagWhat it does
#EXTM3UFile header. Must be the very first line, or players reject the file.
#EXT-X-VERSIONProtocol version. 3+ allows float durations; 7 introduces fMP4 segments.
#EXT-X-TARGETDURATIONRounded maximum segment length in seconds. Players use it to size buffers.
#EXTINFDuration of the segment on the next line.
#EXT-X-MEDIA-SEQUENCESequence number of the first segment — the value that keeps rolling in live windows.
#EXT-X-STREAM-INFDeclares one bitrate/resolution variant in a master playlist.
#EXT-X-ENDLISTMarks the end of the list. Present means VOD; absent means live.
#EXT-X-KEYEncryption method and key URI for the segments that follow.
#EXT-X-DISCONTINUITYWarns that encoding parameters change here — common around ad breaks.
#EXT-X-PLAYLIST-TYPEHints VOD or EVENT, telling the player whether the list may grow.

8. What HLS is not

Three misconceptions cause most of the confusion in bug reports:

  • HLS is not a codec. It says nothing about how pixels are compressed. The video inside is usually H.264 or HEVC and the audio AAC; HLS only describes how those bytes are chopped up and delivered.
  • HLS does not mean lower quality. A 4K HDR master and a 240p mobile copy can live in the same playlist. The protocol adapts; the encoder decides the ceiling.
  • HLS is not encrypted by default. Plain HLS segments are ordinary files anyone can fetch. Protection comes from #EXT-X-KEY (typically AES-128) or from DRM systems layered on top.

9. Where HLS plays without help

Native HLS support, and where a JavaScript library is required
PlatformNative HLS?What that means
Safari on macOS and iOSYesA <video src="…m3u8"> tag just works.
Any browser on iOSYesAll iOS browsers use WebKit, so they inherit the same support.
Chrome, Edge, Firefox on desktopNoNeeds a JavaScript player such as hls.js on top of MSE.
Chrome on AndroidVariesDevice-dependent; never rely on it for a production page.
Smart TVs, Apple TV, Roku, set-top boxesUsuallyHLS is the de facto standard in this category.

This split is why "it plays on my iPhone but not in Chrome" is the single most reported HLS problem. Nothing is broken — the two clients simply implement different parts of the stack.

Frequently asked questions

Is m3u8 a video file?

No. It is a plain-text playlist, usually a few kilobytes, that points at the video segments. Opening it in a text editor shows you the manifest; opening it in a player starts the stream.

Why does Safari play my stream but Chrome shows nothing?

Because Safari has a native HLS implementation and Chrome does not. On Chrome the page must load a JavaScript player that reassembles the segments through Media Source Extensions. If the page has no such player, you get a blank frame rather than an error.

How long should an HLS segment be?

Six seconds is a common modern default, with two to ten seconds the usual range. Shorter segments start faster and adapt quicker but cost more requests; longer segments buffer better and are cheaper to serve, at the price of higher latency.

Can I download an HLS stream to one file?

Technically yes — fetch the playlist, then every segment, then concatenate them. Whether you are allowed to is a separate question: respect the content owner's rights and any DRM in use. This site's player deliberately does not offer a download button.

Does HLS work over HTTPS only?

No, plain HTTP works, but modern browsers block mixed content, so an HLS stream loaded from an HTTPS page must also be HTTPS. In practice, serve everything over HTTPS.
