How these guides are written

Three rules keep them useful. Every claim is tested against a real stream or a real file before it is published. Every procedure ends in something you can check — a command, a header, a number — rather than advice. And nothing is padded: if a topic needs four hundred words, it gets four hundred words.

They are also kept current. When a browser changes how it handles HLS, or a CDN default shifts, the affected guide is updated and the change is noted on the page.

If something here is wrong or out of date, the contact page is the fastest way to tell us — corrections are usually fixed within a week.
