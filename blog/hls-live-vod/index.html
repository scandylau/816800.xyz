HLS 直播与点播：m3u8 的两种用法 HLS Live vs VOD: Two Uses of M3U8

2026-08-28 · 5 min read

一、同一个 m3u8，两种模式

很多人以为 m3u8 只是“点播文件”，其实它既能做点播（VOD）也能做直播（Live）。区别不在文件后缀，而在清单里的“时间轴是否还在生长”。

二、点播（VOD）

清单是完整且固定的——所有分片一次性列全，末尾有 #EXT-X-ENDLIST 标记。播放器知道总长、能拖动进度条、可以任意跳转。普通影视、课程、录播都用这种。

三、直播（Live）

清单持续增长：服务端不断往末尾追加新分片、删除旧分片，#EXT-X-ENDLIST 不存在（或更晚才出现）。播放器定期刷新清单、追着最新的分片播，所以是“跟着时间走”。直播延迟主要来自分片时长与缓冲区。

四、怎么一眼区分

  • 打开你的 m3u8（或粘贴到 在线播放器），看是否含 #EXT-X-ENDLIST。
  • 有 → 点播；没有且画面一直在更新 → 直播。
  • 直播还会带 #EXT-X-MEDIA-SEQUENCE 标明当前窗口的起始序号。

五、给开发者的提示

直播断流时，往往不是“地址失效”，而是服务端停推了——此时清单还在但分片拉不到。这也是为什么 播放失败排查要先区分“地址失效”和“CORS 被拦截”。

1. Same m3u8, two modes

Many think m3u8 is just "VOD", but it does both VOD and Live. The difference isn't the extension but whether the timeline is still growing.

2. VOD

The playlist is complete and fixed — all segments listed at once, ending with #EXT-X-ENDLIST. The player knows the total length, supports seeking and arbitrary jumps. Movies, courses, recordings use this.

3. Live

The playlist keeps growing: the server appends new segments and drops old ones; #EXT-X-ENDLIST is absent (or appears much later). The player periodically refreshes and chases the newest segment, so it "follows time". Latency comes mainly from segment length and buffering.

4. How to tell at a glance

  • Open your m3u8 (or paste into the online player) and look for #EXT-X-ENDLIST.
  • Present → VOD; absent but picture keeps updating → Live.
  • Live also carries #EXT-X-MEDIA-SEQUENCE marking the start index of the current window.

5. A note for developers

When a live stream drops, it's often not a dead URL but the server stopped pushing — the playlist remains but segments can't be fetched. That's why the troubleshooting guide first separates dead URLs from CORS blocks.
