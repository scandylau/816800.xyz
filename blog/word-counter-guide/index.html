一、字数统计能告诉你什么

很多人以为字数统计只是「数有几个词」，其实它能同时给出词数、字符数（含/不含空格）、段落数、句子数，以及最重要的——阅读时长和关键词密度。这些数字对写论文、写 SEO 文案、写社媒帖子都很有用。

二、词数、字符数分别怎么算

  • 词数（Words）：按空格和标点切分后统计的「词」的数量，是英文最常看的指标。
  • 字符数（Characters）：逐字符计数；分「含空格」与「不含空格」两种，写推文、简历时常受字符上限约束。
  • 段落 / 句子数：用于判断文章结构是否过于冗长或碎裂。

三、关键词密度与 SEO

关键词密度 = 某关键词出现次数 ÷ 总词数。它是早期 SEO 的常用参考：密度过低可能「主题不明确」，过高则像堆砌（关键词堆砌会被搜索引擎降权）。一般把核心词密度维持在 1%–2% 附近较为自然，更重要的是语义相关、自然通顺。

四、阅读时长怎么估算

通用经验值：成年人平均阅读速度约 200–250 词/分钟。用「总词数 ÷ 220」即可估算分钟数。本站会直接给出「约 X 分钟阅读」，方便你在标题或开头标注，提升读者预期管理。

五、怎么用本站的字数统计

  2. 粘贴或输入文本，统计即时更新。

1. What a word counter tells you

Many think word counting is just "how many words" — but it also gives word count, character count (with/without spaces), paragraphs and sentences, plus the two that matter most: reading time and keyword density. Those numbers help with essays, SEO copy and social posts alike.

2. How words and characters are counted

  • Words: the number of space/punctuation-delimited tokens — the metric English writing cares about most.
  • Characters: counted per character, usually split into "with spaces" and "without"; handy when a tweet or résumé has a hard limit.
  • Paragraphs / sentences: a quick read on whether structure is too bloated or too fragmented.

3. Keyword density & SEO

Keyword density = (keyword occurrences) ÷ (total words). It's a classic SEO signal: too low can look "off-topic", too high looks like stuffing (which search engines penalize). Keeping a primary term around 1%–2% reads naturally; relevance and fluency matter more than the exact number.

4. Estimating reading time

Rule of thumb: adults read about 200–250 words per minute. Divide total words by 220 to estimate minutes. This site shows "X min read" directly, so you can label posts and set reader expectations.

The 220 wpm figure is a midpoint, not a law. Skimmers move faster, dense technical text slows readers down, and non-native audiences often read 30–40% slower. When precision matters — course material, legal copy, subtitles — time a real reader once and calibrate your own divisor.

5. Limits you should know before you rely on one

Every platform counts differently, and the differences are big enough to cause rejected submissions. The table lists the cases people hit most often:

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a "word"?

Any run of characters separated by spaces or punctuation. "State-of-the-art" is one word; "2026" is one word. Different tools may disagree by a few percent on hyphenated or numeric text — again, the receiving platform's counter is the authority.

Is keyword density still an SEO ranking factor?

Not directly. Modern search engines judge topic coverage and quality, not keyword ratios. Density is still useful as a sanity check: if a term crosses roughly 3% the page usually reads as stuffed, and if your target phrase never appears the page may struggle to rank for it.

How long should a blog post be?

Long enough to cover the topic and no longer. As anchors: a focused how-to lands around 800–1,200 words, a comparison piece 1,200–2,000. Reading time is the better label for readers; word count is the better budget for you.

Does the counter work with languages other than English?

Character and paragraph counts work for any text. Word counting is most accurate for space-delimited languages such as English; for unspaced scripts the character count is the meaningful number.
