How we choose the sites listed here

A link from us is a recommendation, so the bar is higher than "you linked to us first". Every site above was checked against the same four questions:

  • Does it actually work? We open it and use the main feature. Broken flows, dead links or a wall of popups disqualify a site immediately.
  • Is it useful to the same reader? Our audience is developers, streaming engineers and technical writers. A well-made tool or a genuine directory fits; unrelated content does not.
  • Does it respect the visitor? No forced signup just to try the feature, no aggressive ad layouts, and ideally work that stays on the device.
  • Is it maintained? Something touched in the last few months, not a project abandoned years ago.

Want to be added?

Send the site URL and one line on what it does via the contact page. We review requests within one to two weeks and reply either way — a silent no is not how we work.

We do not require a reciprocal link and we do not accept payment for placement. If your site is a good fit we will list it whether or not you link back; if it isn't, no amount of link swapping changes that.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to link back to 816800.xyz?

No. A reciprocal link is welcome but never a condition. We would rather list a genuinely useful site than trade favours.

My site is listed but has changed since then.

Tell us and we will update or remove the entry. Listings are reviewed periodically, but we would rather hear it from you first.

Do you list competitors?

Yes, if they are good. A reader who needs a working tool matters more to us than defending a keyword.

Why are there only two sites here?

Because we would rather have two links we stand behind than twenty that pad the page. The list grows when we find something worth recommending, not on a schedule.
