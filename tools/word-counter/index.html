Why Word Count and Keyword Density Still Matter

Word count shapes how long a reader stays, and keyword density hints at what a page is about. Neither is a ranking factor on its own, but both help you ship clearer writing. This tool measures both instantly, in your browser.

Reading time, explained

The reading estimate uses 200 words per minute, a common benchmark for silent reading in English. Speaking time uses about 130 words per minute, close to a natural pace for presentations. Real readers vary, so treat these as planning numbers rather than promises.

Limits you can check at a glance: a Google meta description truncates around 155 characters, a tweet allows 280 characters, and a single SMS segment holds 160 characters. This tool shows character counts both with and without spaces, so you can match whichever limit applies.

How to read keyword density

Density answers a simple question: how much of this text is about a given word? If a word appears 10 times in a 500 word article, that is 2 percent. The top list below is computed after removing common stop words, so the words that remain usually reflect the actual subject.

There is no perfect percentage. Search engines now model synonyms and context, so repeating a phrase to hit a target number does more harm than good. A natural spread between roughly 0.5 and 2 percent usually reads well and still signals the topic clearly.

A practical workflow

  • Draft the piece for the reader first, then check the numbers.
  • Look at the top list: if your intended topic word is missing, the text probably drifted.
  • If one word dominates far above the rest, rewrite a few sentences with a synonym.
  • Compare against similar pages rather than against an abstract ideal.
The stop word filter removes common English function words such as the, of, and and to. Turn it off when you need raw counts, for example when matching a strict editorial requirement.

Frequently asked questions

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute and speaking time at about 130 words per minute. These are widely used averages, so treat them as estimates.

What is keyword density?

It is the percentage of total words that a given word represents. A word appearing 10 times in a 500 word article has a density of 2 percent.

What is a good keyword density for SEO?

There is no official ideal figure. Write naturally for readers first; roughly 0.5 to 2 percent usually signals relevance without hurting readability.

Does this tool count characters with and without spaces?

Yes, both figures are shown so you can check limits such as meta descriptions, tweets or SMS messages.

Is my text stored or uploaded anywhere?

No. All counting happens locally in your browser with JavaScript, and nothing is saved after you close the tab.

为什么字数与关键词密度仍然重要

字数影响读者的停留时长，关键词密度反映页面的主题倾向。两者都不是直接的排名因素，但都能帮助你写出更清晰的内容。本工具在你的浏览器里即时给出这两组数据。

阅读时长是怎么算的

阅读时长按每分钟 200 词估算，这是英文默读的常用基准；朗读时长按每分钟约 130 词估算，接近演讲的自然语速。真实读者差异很大，请把它当作规划参考而非精确值。

几个常见上限可以对照：Google 的 meta description 约 155 字符后会被截断，一条推文上限 280 字符，单条短信为 160 字符。工具同时给出含空格与不含空格的字符数，方便你匹配对应限制。

怎么理解关键词密度

密度回答的是一个简单问题：这篇内容有多大比例在讲某个词？一个词在 500 词文章中出现 10 次，密度就是 2%。下方排行已剔除常见停用词，因此留下的词通常更贴近真实主题。

不存在完美的百分比。搜索引擎已经能理解同义词与上下文，为了凑数字而堆砌关键词往往得不偿失。通常 0.5%–2% 的自然分布既好读，也能清楚传达主题。

推荐的使用流程

  • 先面向读者写完初稿，再看数据。
  • 看顶部排行：如果你想主打的主题词没出现，说明内容可能跑题了。
  • 如果某个词明显高于其他词，用同义词改写几句。
  • 与同类页面横向比较，而不是对照某个抽象标准。
停用词过滤会去掉 the、of、and、to 等英文常见功能词。当你需要原始计数（例如满足严格的编辑规范）时，可以关闭这个开关。

常见问题

阅读时长是怎么计算的？

阅读时长按每分钟 200 词估算，朗读时长按每分钟约 130 词估算，均为常用平均值，请作为参考。

什么是关键词密度？

它是某个词在全文词数中所占的百分比。一个词在 500 词文章中出现 10 次，密度为 2%。

SEO 的理想关键词密度是多少？

没有官方标准。请优先为读者自然写作，通常 0.5%–2% 的分布既能体现相关性，又不影响可读性。

字符数会区分是否含空格吗？

会。工具同时给出含空格与不含空格两个数值，方便你核对 meta description、推文或短信等限制。
