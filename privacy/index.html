Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

816800.xyz（以下简称「本站」）高度重视你的隐私。本政策说明我们在你使用本站工具时如何处理数据。

1. 我们收集什么

几乎不收集。本站所有工具均为纯前端实现，你输入的流媒体地址、待处理文本或文件不会发送或被上传到任何服务器，仅在你本地浏览器中完成解析与处理。

2. 本地存储（localStorage）

为方便你复测，M3U8 测试页会在你的浏览器 localStorage 中保存最近 5 条测试地址。这些数据：

  • 仅存储于你当前设备与浏览器，我们服务器端无法读取；
  • 你可以在测试历史区逐条删除，或清空浏览器数据一并移除；
  • 绝不会用于广告投放或用户画像。

3. 日志与匿名统计

托管方（Cloudflare Pages）可能记录标准访问日志（如 IP、User-Agent、访问时间）用于安全防护与流量统计。这些数据由托管方按行业惯例处理，我们不以此识别个人身份。

4. Cookie 与追踪

本站自身不设置追踪 Cookie。首次访问会出现同意横幅，提供「接受」与「拒绝」两个并列选项：在你作出选择之前，不加载任何统计脚本、不写入 Cookie、不发送请求；选择「拒绝」则永久不加载。选择结果记录在浏览器本地，清除本站数据即可撤回。

Google Analytics 4（衡量 ID：G-JEMKSVKD32）仅在你点击「接受」后才加载，用于统计访问量与页面使用情况。若未来启用广告（如 Google AdSense），第三方厂商可能使用 Cookie 投放广告；我们将仅以手动方式在指定位置放置广告单元，并尽最大努力保护你的浏览体验。

5. 第三方内容

当你使用 M3U8 播放器加载外部流地址时，视频数据由该流媒体服务器直接提供，其隐私实践由其运营方负责，请自行确认所加载来源的合规性。

6. 儿童隐私

本站不面向 13 岁以下儿童，亦不主动收集其信息。

7. 联系我们

如对本政策有疑问，请通过 联系页 与我们沟通。

816800.xyz (the "Site") takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data is handled when you use our tools, why it is handled, and the rights you have over it. It is written to satisfy the transparency requirements of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (CCPA/CPRA).

1. Who we are

The Site is a static toolkit operated by a small independent team. For any question about this policy, write to admin@816800.xyz or use the contact page. Because we run no user accounts, in most cases we hold no data about you at all — which makes requests quick to satisfy.

2. What we collect

Almost nothing. Every tool here is pure front-end. The stream URLs, text or files you enter are never sent to or uploaded to any server — parsing and processing happen entirely inside your own browser.

The only "personal data" the Site routinely processes, in the GDPR sense, is the technical information in server logs described in section 4 — typically an IP address. We do not ask for your name, email, payment details or precise location. If you choose to email us, we hold only what you sent, for as long as it takes to answer.

3. Local storage (localStorage)

To help you re-test, the M3U8 page stores your last 5 tested URLs in your browser's localStorage. This data:

  • lives only on your current device and browser; our servers cannot read it;
  • can be deleted one by one in the history area, or removed entirely by clearing browser data;
  • is never used for advertising or user profiling.

No cookie consent is required for this storage because it is strictly necessary to deliver a feature you explicitly used, and it never leaves your device.

4. Logs & analytics

Our host (Cloudflare Pages) keeps standard access logs — IP address, user agent, requested path and timestamp — for security, abuse prevention and traffic statistics. We also measure aggregate usage with Google Analytics 4, which relies on cookies and may process your IP address in truncated form. Analytics tell us which pages are worth improving; they are not used to build individual profiles or to make automated decisions about you.

5. Cookies, advertising and opting out

The Site sets no tracking cookies of its own. Third parties that appear on the Site may set their own:

Your choice comes first

On your first visit, a consent banner gives you two equally prominent options — Accept or Reject. Until you choose, nothing is measured: no analytics script is loaded, no cookie is written and no request is sent.

Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID G-JEMKSVKD32) is loaded only after you accept. We use it to count visits and see which pages get used. Before any consent is given, analytics is technically blocked rather than merely opted out — the script is never requested.

You can block analytics across all sites by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or by refusing cookies in your browser.

Third-party advertising

When advertising is enabled on the Site, third-party vendors — including Google — may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time:

  • Google: Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com)
  • Industry-wide: aboutads.info choices page, or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance page at youronlinechoices.eu

Opting out does not remove advertising; it stops ads from being personalised using cookies. We place ad units manually and keep them to a minimum so they never interfere with using a tool.

6. Third-party content

When you load an external stream in the M3U8 player, the video data is served directly by that streaming server; its privacy practices are the responsibility of its operator, and that operator may see your IP address. Please verify the compliance of any source you load, especially on a network you do not control.

8. Your rights under the GDPR / UK GDPR

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to:

  • Be informed — this document is that notice.

  • Erasure — have your data deleted where there is no overriding reason to keep it.
  • Restrict or object to processing, including profiling.
  • Data portability — receive data you provided in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

To exercise any of these, email admin@816800.xyz. We reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

9. Your California rights (CCPA / CPRA)

You are entitled to equal service and price regardless of whether you exercise these rights.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes other than those permitted by the statute. We have no "Do Not Sell" toggle because there is nothing to opt out of. To make a request, email admin@816800.xyz; we verify requests by replying to the address you use.
