Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

816800.xyz（以下简称「本站」）高度重视你的隐私。本政策说明我们在你使用本站工具时如何处理数据。

1. 我们收集什么

几乎不收集。本站所有工具均为纯前端实现，你输入的流媒体地址、待处理文本或文件不会发送或被上传到任何服务器，仅在你本地浏览器中完成解析与处理。

2. 本地存储（localStorage）

为方便你复测，M3U8 测试页会在你的浏览器 localStorage 中保存最近 5 条测试地址。这些数据：

  • 仅存储于你当前设备与浏览器，我们服务器端无法读取；
  • 你可以在测试历史区逐条删除，或清空浏览器数据一并移除；
  • 绝不会用于广告投放或用户画像。

3. 日志与匿名统计

托管方（Cloudflare Pages）可能记录标准访问日志（如 IP、User-Agent、访问时间）用于安全防护与流量统计。这些数据由托管方按行业惯例处理，我们不以此识别个人身份。

4. Cookie 与追踪

本站不设置追踪 Cookie，不接入第三方行为分析脚本。若未来启用广告（如 Google AdSense），我们将仅以手动方式在指定位置放置广告单元，并尽最大努力保护你的浏览体验。

5. 第三方内容

当你使用 M3U8 播放器加载外部流地址时，视频数据由该流媒体服务器直接提供，其隐私实践由其运营方负责，请自行确认所加载来源的合规性。

6. 儿童隐私

本站不面向 13 岁以下儿童，亦不主动收集其信息。

7. 联系我们

如对本政策有疑问，请通过 联系页 与我们沟通。

816800.xyz (hereinafter "the Site") takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle data when you use our tools.

1. What we collect

Almost nothing. Every tool here is pure front-end. The stream URLs, text or files you enter are never sent to or uploaded to any server — parsing and processing happen entirely in your local browser.

2. Local storage (localStorage)

To help you re-test, the M3U8 page stores your last 5 tested URLs in your browser's localStorage. This data:

  • lives only on your current device and browser; our servers cannot read it;
  • can be deleted one by one in the history area, or removed entirely by clearing browser data;
  • is never used for advertising or user profiling.

3. Logs & anonymous stats

The host (Cloudflare Pages) may keep standard access logs (e.g. IP, User-Agent, time) for security and traffic statistics, handled per industry practice; we do not use them to identify individuals.

4. Cookies & tracking

The Site sets no tracking cookies and loads no third-party behavioral analytics. If ads are enabled later (e.g. Google AdSense), we will place ad units manually at designated spots only, and do our best to protect your browsing experience.

5. Third-party content

When you load an external stream in the M3U8 player, the video data is served directly by that streaming server; its privacy practices are the responsibility of its operator. Please verify the compliance of any source you load.

6. Children's privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, please reach us via the contact page.
