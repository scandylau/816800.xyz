报告里请带上这些

修复慢的头号原因是问题无法复现。把这份清单复制进邮件正文：

  1. 哪个工具或页面，有完整 URL 更好。
  2. 具体的输入——流地址、粘贴的文本或文件类型。

常见问题

我的消息发到哪里去了？

在你点击发送之前，它哪儿也没去。本站是纯静态站、没有后端，表单只是在你自己的邮件客户端里拼好一封草稿，收件人是 admin@816800.xyz。没有任何内容写入数据库，在你选择发送之前我们也看不到你的邮箱地址。

你们提供付费支持或定制工具吗？

暂时不提供。工具完全免费，支持是尽力而为。如果你需要合同级别的响应时间，我们不是合适的对象。

能帮我播别人的流吗？

我们可以在技术层面解释一条流为什么会失败——CORS 头、不支持的编码、过期的令牌、地区封锁——但不会帮你绕过不属于你的内容的访问控制或 DRM。

接受投稿或付费链接吗？

不接受。站内每篇指南都由我们自己撰写，也不出售链接。与真实在用的站点互换友链是欢迎的，细节见友链页。

What we can help with

  • Bug reports — a tool returned the wrong result, froze, or failed to load a stream.
  • Stream debugging — an M3U8 or MPD URL refuses to play and you want a second opinion on whether the manifest, the codec or CORS is at fault.
  • Feature requests — a tool you wish existed, or an option missing from one that does.
  • Content corrections — something in a guide is out of date, inaccurate or missing a step.
  • Link exchange and partnerships — see the criteria on our friends page.

What to include so we can actually help

The single biggest cause of a slow fix is a report we cannot reproduce. Copy this checklist into your message:

  1. Which tool or page, with the full URL if you have it.
  2. The exact input — the stream address, the pasted text, or the file type.
  3. What you expected, and what actually happened.
  4. Your browser and operating system, for example "Firefox 141 on Windows 11".
  5. Any error text from the page or the browser console.

For stream problems, the M3U8 player prints a built-in diagnostic that separates "URL unreachable" from "blocked by CORS". Pasting that line into your message saves a full round trip.

Frequently asked questions

Where does my message go?

Nowhere until you send it. This is a static site with no back end, so the form only assembles a draft inside your own email client, addressed to admin@816800.xyz. Nothing is written to a database, and we never see your address until you choose to send.

Do you offer paid support or custom tools?

Not at the moment. The toolkit is free and support is best-effort. If you need contractual response times, we are the wrong contact.

Can you help me play a stream that isn't mine?

We can explain why a stream fails at a technical level — CORS headers, unsupported codecs, expired tokens, geo-blocking — but we will not help circumvent access controls or DRM on content you do not own.

Do you accept guest posts or paid links?

No. Every guide on this site is written by us and we do not sell links. Genuine link swaps with sites we actually use are welcome; details are on the friends page.
