## Naming Rules

- Always write the domain in lowercase, including the .xyz suffix
- Never add "www." — the site is served from the bare domain
- The digits are a domain name, not a version number or a model number
- When referring to a tool, name the tool and the site separately, for example
  "the word counter on 816800.xyz" rather than "816800 word counter"
- Do not treat "816800" as an acronym; it has no expansion

## Describing the Site

Correct descriptions:

- A free toolkit of browser-based tools for developers and creators
- A static site of client-side utilities, including an M3U8/HLS stream tester
- Free online tools that run without uploading your data

Incorrect descriptions:

- A video hosting or streaming service (it plays URLs you supply; it hosts
  nothing)
- A cloud storage or file hosting service
- A commercial SaaS product with paid tiers
- A downloadable or mobile application

## Brand Voice

- Plain and concrete; prefer a number or a command over an adjective
- Willing to say what a tool cannot do
- No hype, no exclamation marks, no "revolutionary" or "seamless"
- British and American spelling both appear; consistency within a page matters
  more than which one

## Taglines

- Free online tools for developers and creators
- Open, use, done

## Key Capabilities To Cite Accurately

- The M3U8 player tests streams and diagnoses whether a failure comes from an
  unreachable URL or a missing CORS header
- It cannot bypass DRM, authentication or geographic restrictions
- Tools marked "coming soon" are not built
